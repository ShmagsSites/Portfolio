What We Fix

Every Plumbing Problem,
Handled.

Whether it's a dripping faucet or a flooded basement, our licensed team handles residential and light commercial plumbing throughout Portage County.

Emergency

Burst Pipes & Flooding

Water pouring where it shouldn't be? Call us now. We have 24/7 emergency crews ready to respond and stop the damage fast.

Drain Cleaning

Slow drains, full clogs, recurring backups. We use professional-grade augers and hydro-jetting to clear the line completely, not just push it down.

Leak Repair

Leaky faucets, running toilets, dripping supply lines, slab leaks. We find it, quote it, and fix it, often in a single visit.

Water Heater Service

Tank and tankless water heater repair, replacement, and installation. We carry common tank sizes on the truck for same-day installs.

Toilet Repair & Replacement

Running, rocking, not flushing, or leaking at the base, we repair all brands and can replace with a new unit the same day.

Remodel & New Installs

Bathroom or kitchen remodel? Adding a laundry room? We rough-in and finish plumbing for new construction and renovation projects of all sizes.
