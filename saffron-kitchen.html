Our Story

Saffron Kitchen was born from a single conviction: that the flavors of the Indian subcontinent deserve the same reverence as any fine cuisine in the world. Our chef-owner, Arjun Mehta, grew up in Mumbai before training under Michelin-starred chefs in London and New York.

"Food is memory. Every spice in my kitchen carries a story from my mother's hands."

We source our spices directly from small farms in Kerala, Rajasthan, and Jammu, ground fresh each morning in our kitchen. Nothing here comes from a jar.
