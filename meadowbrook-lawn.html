What We Do

Complete Lawn Care,
Done Right.

From weekly mowing to full-season fertilization programs, our licensed crews handle every inch of your property, with the equipment, training, and accountability that DIY and cut-rate outfits simply can't match.

Most Popular

Lawn Mowing & Edging

Weekly or bi-weekly cuts, clean line edging along all borders, and blow-down of all hard surfaces after every visit. We mow at the right height for your grass type, not just the fastest pass.

  • Height calibrated per grass species
  • Sharp commercial blades, no tearing
  • Clippings dispersed or bagged on request
  • Consistent crew assigned to your route

Fertilization & Weed Control

A 5-round annual program calibrated to Northeast Ohio's soil and climate. We pull a soil sample before round one so every application targets your lawn's actual deficiencies, not a generic formula.

  • Soil test included, every season
  • Pre- and post-emergent weed control
  • Slow-release nitrogen blends
  • Organic upgrade available

Core Aeration & Overseeding

Compacted soil is the #1 reason Ohio lawns thin out over time. Our core aerators pull actual plugs from the soil, not spike rolls, allowing water, air, and seed direct root contact.

  • Deep-tine core aeration (not spike)
  • Premium tall fescue / bluegrass seed blends
  • Best done late August – October in OH
  • Follow-up moisture check included
Seasonal Priority

Spring & Fall Cleanup

Leaf removal, debris hauling, bed edging, and turf cleanup. We don't just blow everything to the curb, we haul it off. Spring visits also include a post-winter lawn damage assessment at no extra charge.

  • Full leaf removal & haul-off
  • Bed clearing & re-edge
  • Post-winter turf damage report
Spring Service

Mulching & Bed Maintenance

Fresh mulch installation, bed weeding, edging definition, and annual renewal, the fastest way to make your whole property look professionally maintained between lawn visits.

  • Double or triple-shred hardwood mulch
  • Pre-emergent weed barrier install
  • Crisp edging along all bed borders

Shrub & Hedge Trimming

Precision trimming of ornamentals, hedges, and foundation shrubs. We use hand shears for delicate specimens and don't "top" plants, proper form preserves plant health and keeps the look clean through the season.

  • Timed to bloom cycles, not calendars
  • All debris removed same day
  • Shrub health check at every visit
