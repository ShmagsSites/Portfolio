What We Handle

Residential & Commercial
Electrical Services

Every job, big or small, is done by a licensed electrician with the experience to get it right and the integrity to tell you what you actually need.

Emergency

Power Outages & Tripped Breakers

Partial or full power loss? We diagnose the cause fast and restore power safely, same day, any day of the week including holidays.

Panel Upgrades & Replacements

Outdated 100-amp panels, Federal Pacific or Zinsco panels, or panels that just can't keep up, we upgrade to modern 200-amp service safely and to code.

Outlet, Switch & Fixture Installs

Adding outlets, replacing switches, installing ceiling fans, recessed lighting, or exterior fixtures. Clean work, no shortcuts, everything to code.

Whole-Home Rewiring

Older home with knob-and-tube or aluminum wiring? We safely replace it with modern copper wiring, protecting your home and your insurance coverage.

Surge Protection

Whole-home surge protectors guard your appliances and electronics from damaging power spikes. One install, everything protected.

Safety Inspections & Permits

Buying or selling a home? We perform electrical safety inspections, pull permits for all permitted work, and provide documentation for real estate closings.

EV Charger Installation

Level 1 or Level 2 home charging stations installed correctly, up to code, and in the location that works best for your garage or driveway.

Commercial & Light Industrial

Tenant buildouts, equipment connections, lighting retrofits, and more. We work around your schedule so you stay open and operational.
