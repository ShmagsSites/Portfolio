Book Your Visit

We're a by-appointment studio — this ensures every guest receives our complete, unhurried attention. Request your visit below and we'll confirm within a few hours.

New guests receive a complimentary 15-minute consultation before any service begins.
A credit card is required to hold your appointment. No charge until your visit.
We ask for 48 hours' notice for cancellations to avoid a 50% late-cancel fee.
Color correction and extension consultations are required before scheduling the full service.
Children under 12 must be accompanied by a parent for the duration of the visit.
