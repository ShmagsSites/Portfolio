Since 1937

A Kent Institution

Since 1937, Ray's Place has been the heartbeat of downtown Kent, a gathering place for students, locals, and legends alike. Over eight decades, it has outlasted everything: recessions, pandemics, and trends that came and went. Ray's just keeps going.

"Hundreds of students have worked there, thousands of customers have dined there, and millions of glasses have been raised there."

Meet Me at Ray's, by Patrick J. O'Connor
