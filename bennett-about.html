Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is title insurance and why do I need it?+
Title insurance is a form of indemnity insurance that protects the holder from financial loss sustained from defects in title to a property. It means that Bennett Land Title will research the title to make sure you're getting your property free and clear of any title issues and we will guarantee it with insurance so you don't have to worry about it in the future. That means your family or your business is safe!
Can I choose my own title company?+
Yes, you can choose your title company. Call us at our Brimfield or Ravenna branches and you'll see how easy it can be with Bennett Land Title Agency.
Do you handle closings outside of business hours?+
Yes. We even work nights and weekends when it works best for you. We have two offices for convenience but we don't require you to come to one of them, we can meet you anywhere.
What counties do you serve in Ohio?+
We're in all of Ohio's 88 counties. While our two offices are located in Brimfield and Ravenna, we handle title work across the entire state of Ohio.
How much does title insurance cost?+
The amount of liability of a policy of title insurance will depend upon the type of coverage. Typically, the owner's title policy is based on the purchase price of the property. Contact us and we'll walk you through exactly what to expect for your transaction.

Still have questions?

Give us a call or send us an email. We're happy to answer any questions about the closing process, title insurance, or what to expect on closing day.
