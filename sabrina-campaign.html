Meet Sabrina

A neighbor, a business owner, and a public servant who listens.

For 36 years, Portage County has been the place I'm proud to call home — a great place to work, shop, run a business, go to church, and raise a family. As a local small business owner, I understand what it takes to meet a payroll, balance a budget, and serve people well.

I believe the most critical quality of any effective leader is the willingness and ability to listen. That's why my door is always open and I remain accessible to my constituents through every means of communication. I'm committed to being an effective leader in helping us build a financially sound county government — one rooted in accountability and responsible stewardship of taxpayer dollars.

Sabrina Christian-BennettPortage County Commissioner

What I Stand For

Priorities for Portage County

Practical, accountable leadership focused on the issues that matter most to the families and businesses of our county.

Fiscal Responsibility

Building an effective and financially sound county government through disciplined budgeting, accountability, and responsible stewardship of every taxpayer dollar.

Open & Accessible Government

Keeping the door open to every resident. Real leadership starts with listening — and being reachable through every means of communication, every day.

Economic Growth & Jobs

Supporting local small businesses and a strong economy so Portage County remains a great place to work, shop, and build a livelihood for generations.

Strong Communities

Championing the neighborhoods, churches, and families that make Portage County home — and representing the interests of the entire county, not just a few.
